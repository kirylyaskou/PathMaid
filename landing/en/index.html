01 / Reference

“Just a moment,
I’ll find it.”

Keep the reference library on your desk. Find a rule, spell, or creature quickly and get back to the game.

  • Creatures, spells, and items
  • Conditions, hazards, actions, and rules
  • Search the downloaded database offline

02 / Combat tracker

The fight is yours.
The bookkeeping is mine.

Build an encounter and run combat in one place. Track turns, HP, and effect durations while you narrate the action.

  • Initiative order and rounds
  • HP, damage, healing, and saves
  • Conditions and effect durations
  • Weaknesses, resistances, and immunities
  • Weak and elite opponents

03 / Pathbuilder import

Every hero.
The whole party.
Already here.

Your players build characters in Pathbuilder. Bring them into PathMaid so their details sit beside the encounter, without retyping character sheets.

  • Import finished characters
  • Party details in the GM workspace
  • Heroes beside their foes in combat

04 / Campaign manager

This story
continues.

Keep campaign notes, documents, images, and their connections together. Returning for the next session is easier when everything has its place.

  • Campaign materials and notes
  • Links between documents
  • Images and handouts

Campaign management is still evolving. GM feedback helps shape what comes next.

05 / Creature builder

Made to
your order.

When the bestiary cannot keep up with your imagination, make your own creatures, edit their statistics, and save them for future encounters.

  • Your creatures and their statistics
  • A personal collection of foes
  • Import and export creatures

03 / Time for the story

Less bookkeeping.
More adventure.

Have notes, rules, and encounters ready when the game begins. You are still the one telling the story.

04 / Cloud sync

Another computer.
The same campaign.

Prep on one device and run the game on another. Sign in to keep campaigns, encounters, custom creatures, notes, and images close at hand.

  • Materials and images move with the campaign
